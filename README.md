# Simply Light — Decorate Builds Without Torches

Simply Light 9minecraft Mod gives you clean and colorful ways to light up your builds. It adds glowing blocks, lamps, and thin light strips that replace boring standard torches. You get plenty of options to decorate your rooms, paths, and walls without needing any complicated redstone.

Toggleable Lights: Interact with any light block to turn it on or off quickly. Stops monsters from spawning by giving off a strong, bright glow.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Flanks255**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
